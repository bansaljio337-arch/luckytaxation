GST Registration – Frequently Asked Questions

GST registration kya hai?

GST registration ke baad business ko GSTIN milta hai, jisse woh GST collect kar sakta hai aur input tax credit claim kar sakta hai.

GST registration kiske liye zaroori hai?

Aam taur par goods supplier ke liye ₹40 lakh aur service provider ke liye ₹20 lakh se zyada turnover par (kuch special category states mein limit kam hoti hai). Kuch cases mein turnover se fark nahi padta, jaise e-commerce ke zariye bechna. Apni sthiti official GST portal par ya professional se confirm karein.

Kya GST registration ki sarkari fee hai?

Nahi. Official portal par GST registration free hai. Luckytaxation ki fee sirf documents check karne, application file karne aur ARN tracking jaisi assistance service ke liye hai.

GST registration ke liye kaun se documents chahiye?

PAN, Aadhaar, photo, business address proof, bank account proof, aur firm/company ho toh uske constitution documents. Poori list blog mein dekhein.

GST registration mein kitna time lagta hai?

Complete documents milne ke baad hum aam taur par 48 ghante mein application file karte hain. GSTIN department approve karta hai, jisme aam taur par kuch working days lagte hain; yeh hamare control mein nahi hai.

Kya ghar ke address par GST registration ho sakta hai?

Aam taur par haan, agar aapke paas us address ka valid proof hai (jaise bijli bill aur zarurat ho toh owner ka consent/NOC). Final decision department ka hota hai.

Kya turnover limit se kam par bhi GST le sakte hain?

Haan, voluntary registration ho sakta hai. Lekin uske baad GST rules aur returns follow karne honge.

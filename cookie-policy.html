What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

How we use them

  • Essential: keep the site and your OTP/payment session working.
  • Analytics: understand which pages are useful (for example Google Analytics, if enabled).
  • Advertising: measure Google Ads performance (for example the gclid parameter and Google Ads tags, if enabled).

Third-party cookies

Payment gateways, Google and similar providers may set their own cookies. We do not control them; please read their policies.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may stop working.
